Leo: privacy

Leo is a private notification bot used only by Phillip Collins. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it handles: summaries of my own email (sender, subject and a short preview), my calendar, my task lists, the status of my own code projects, and status reports from my own computers. It also fetches public weather and market figures.

Where it goes: from my Microsoft 365 account to a private computer in my home, and then to my own Microsoft Teams chat. Microsoft handles messages in Teams under its own terms.

What it does not do: it does not share, sell or send this information to any other person or service, and it is not used for advertising.

Storage: it keeps only a small record of when it last ran, on my own computer.
